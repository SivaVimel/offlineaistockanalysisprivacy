Privacy Policy

Nothing about you is collected.

No account. No analytics. No advertising identifier. No server belonging to this app. Your watchlists, your holdings and what you looked at never leave your phone.

Last updated 17 September 2026

The short version. The App Store label says Data Not Collected, and that is literally true. The app asks public services for public information about a company, by ticker. It never tells them who is asking, and it never tells anyone what you own.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app knows lives in its own private container on your device: your watchlists, your holdings, your saved screens and notes, and the filings and prices it has downloaded. These are ordinary files, encrypted at rest by iOS, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no sync. The data is on your phone and nowhere else.

What leaves your device

Only requests for public information, each one identifying a company and nothing else:

SEC EDGAR

data.sec.gov

A company's own filings — the XBRL from its 10-K, 10-Q and 20-F, its insider Form 4s and, for funds, Form N-PORT. Public record.

Public price feed

Delayed end-of-day closes

Daily closes, dividends and splits for a ticker. Delayed, never live quotes, and labelled as delayed everywhere they appear.

Those services see a request for a public company from an ordinary IP address, with no identifier attached. They are not told who you are, what you hold, or what else you have looked at.

What is never sent

  • Your identity — there is no account, no email, no sign-in.
  • Your holdings, their sizes, or what you paid.
  • Your watchlists, screens, notes or search history.
  • Any device or advertising identifier, or any fingerprint of your phone.

The network connection itself

The app uses an ephemeral network session: no cookies are stored or sent, no credentials are kept, and everything is discarded when the app closes. A data source cannot recognise your device tomorrow as the one that asked today.

The AI runs on your phone

The language model is bundled inside the app and runs entirely on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere for it to work, and it works with no connection at all.

You may optionally connect your own third-party AI service in Settings by supplying your own API key. If — and only if — you do that, the computed figures for the company on screen are sent to that provider so it can phrase them. Those are the same numbers already visible to you. Your watchlists, holdings and history are still never sent. Your key is stored on your device only. This is off by default and the app is fully functional without it.

Tracking and third parties

None. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting to a third party, no attribution framework, no advertising network and no tracking of any kind. The app does not ask for the App Tracking Transparency permission because it has nothing to track you with.

Changes
